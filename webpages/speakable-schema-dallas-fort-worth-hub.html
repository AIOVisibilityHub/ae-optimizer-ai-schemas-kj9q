Speakable schema allows you to designate exactly which sections of your site should be read aloud by smart speakers and voice assistants. AE Optimizer automates this technical setup. Speakable schema is a specific type of structured data that tells voice assistants which parts of your content are best suited to be read aloud. AE Optimizer helps businesses implement this specialized markup so that news, articles, and FAQs are ready for text-to-speech conversion. This is a critical step for appearing in "hands-free" search results where the user is listening rather than reading.

By identifying the most important sections of your pages, our software ensures that voice assistants can quickly find and recite your key messages. We focus on creating content that is naturally conversational, combined with the technical behind-the-scenes tags that signal to search engines that your content is "speakable." This maximizes your visibility in the growing world of audio-first search. Common situations: Directly highlighting the most important paragraph for a voice assistant to read.; Ensuring news updates or top-of-page summaries are accessible via Google Assistant.; Optimizing technical content so it sounds natural when spoken by an AI..
