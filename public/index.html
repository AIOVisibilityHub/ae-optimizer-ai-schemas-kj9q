About AE Optimizer

AE Optimizer is an Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) software designed to make websites visible to AI platforms like Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity, and AI Voice systems like Siri and Alexa and Google AI Assistant. It generates structured data, AI visibility files for websites, and citable content, along with building an AI crawlable website on Github, distributing to other highly trusted platforms in order to create an authority network across the web.

Published resources cover Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) software, AI Visibility website optimization, Structured Data generation, Knowledge Graph and Entity File generation, AI-Crawlable Website Creation, Per-page JSON-LD website schema generation, AI citations generation, answer engine optimization for websites voice search, voice search optimization, how to optimize for voice search, and 74 additional service areas.

Service coverage includes Los Angeles, San Diego, Austin, Denver, Salt Lake City, Las Vegas, San Francisco , Atlanta, Washington D.C., Columbus and 10 more communities.
